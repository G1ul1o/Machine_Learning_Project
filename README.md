# Machine_Learning_Project : Climate Change Trends Analysis
## Module : Machine Learning

During our semester, we had to complete a project in Machine Learning. We were free to choose our topic, and our group chose the following problem:

**Climate Change Trends Analysis: Use time-series analysis on temperature, CO₂ concentration, and sea-level datasets to predict future climate change trends.**

For this project, we had to select datasets ourselves. In our case, we chose three datasets (as you can see further in the README) that we merged together.

## Context
- Since 1880, the annual global temperature has risen at an average rate of 0.08°C per decade, becoming a major environmental problem.
- The rate of warming since 1982 is more than twice as fast: 0.20°C per decade.
- Global mean sea level has risen about 21–24 centimeters since 1880. The rising water level is mostly due to a combination of meltwater from glaciers and ice sheets, and thermal expansion of seawater as it warms.

## Objectives
- Visualize the evolution of climate change
- Predict future climate change trends
- Understand the situation and raise awareness

## Datasets
- [Daily Temperature of Major Cities](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sudalairajkumar/daily-temperature-of-major-cities)
- [Global Sea Level 1993 - 2021](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kkhandekar/global-sea-level-1993-2021)
- [Daily atmosphere carbon dioxide concentration](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arunavsutar/daily-atmosphere-carbon-dioxide-concentration)

Note → For the "Daily Temperature of Major Cities" dataset, there is a problem with the Kaggle API in Jupyter. If you use it, you need to download it manually and place it in the same folder as your notebook.
